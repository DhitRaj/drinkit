# 11. Animations

## 1. Motion Principles

- Motion should clarify state, not decorate the UI.
- Use subtle transitions for premium feel.
- Keep critical actions fast and predictable.

## 2. Recommended Motion Patterns

- Screen transitions: short fade and slide.
- Add to cart: quick confirmation with gentle emphasis.
- OTP verification: immediate success feedback.
- Tracking updates: lightweight status changes, no distracting motion.

## 3. Do Not

- Do not use long looping animations.
- Do not animate legal blockers in a playful way.
- Do not make payment or verification states feel uncertain.
