# 02. Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Summary

Drinkit is a hyperlocal alcohol delivery platform for legally eligible customers in permitted Indian markets. The platform includes Customer App, Store Panel, Delivery Partner App, and Admin Panel.

## 2. Goals

- Enable verified adults to order eligible products quickly.
- Route each order to an eligible store with live stock.
- Support 30-45 minute delivery where legally and operationally possible.
- Enforce age verification, state rules, sale hours, and dry-day restrictions.
- Give operators control over inventory, fulfillment, settlements, and compliance.

## 3. MVP Scope

### Customer App
- Mobile OTP signup and login.
- Age verification and identity checks.
- Location-based serviceability and legal gating.
- Category browsing, search, product listing, PDP, cart, checkout, payment, order tracking, order history, support, and profile.

### Store Panel
- Order acceptance and rejection.
- Product availability and price updates.
- Store hours, license, and compliance details.
- Basic analytics and payout visibility.

### Delivery Partner App
- Login, duty toggle, assigned order list, pickup workflow, navigation, delivery confirmation, doorstep ID verification, earnings, and trip history.

### Admin Panel
- Store onboarding, user review, compliance configuration, coupon controls, issue resolution, reports, and analytics.

## 4. Non-Goals for MVP

- Cross-state expansion without legal approval.
- Advanced loyalty program.
- Subscription commerce.
- Marketplace ad platform at scale.
- Multi-warehouse inventory ownership by Drinkit.

## 5. Success Criteria

- Verified signup completion rate above target.
- First order conversion within 7 days.
- Delivery SLA and acceptance SLA within agreed thresholds.
- Doorstep ID verification must never be bypassed.
- Refund, cancellation, and compliance failure rates must stay within guardrails.

## 6. Release Strategy

- Phase 0: Design validation and internal prototype.
- Phase 1: Single-city controlled launch.
- Phase 2: Multi-store, multi-zone expansion.
- Phase 3: Advanced analytics, payouts, loyalty, and brand growth.
