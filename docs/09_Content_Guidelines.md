# 09. Content Guidelines

## 1. Voice

- Clear, premium, and direct.
- Helpful before persuasive.
- No slang-heavy copy in legal or payment surfaces.

## 2. Copy Rules

- Always explain why a block happened.
- Tell the user the next valid action.
- Keep buttons short and action-oriented.
- Avoid legal ambiguity in age, delivery, and payment messages.

## 3. Compliance Microcopy

- Age gate must be explicit.
- Dry-state and sale-hour restrictions must use plain language.
- Doorstep ID failure must explain that the order cannot be handed over.
