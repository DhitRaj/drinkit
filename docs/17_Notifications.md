# 17. Notifications

## 1. Channels

- Push notifications.
- SMS for critical OTP and transactional events.
- WhatsApp where policy allows.
- Email for receipts and reports.

## 2. Message Types

- OTP and login.
- Order placement and delivery status.
- Store acceptance and partner assignment.
- Refund and support updates.
- Compliance and account alerts.

## 3. Rules

- Transactional messages must be reliable and concise.
- Promotional messages must respect consent and channel rules.
- All templates must be reviewable from admin.
