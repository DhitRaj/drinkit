# 05. User Flows

## 1. Customer Flow

1. Open app.
2. Sign up with mobile OTP.
3. Complete age and identity verification.
4. Allow location access and confirm serviceability.
5. Browse categories or search products.
6. Open product detail and add items to cart.
7. Review cart, apply coupon, and choose delivery address.
8. Pay and place order.
9. Track store acceptance, rider pickup, and delivery.
10. Confirm delivery, rate order, and view history.

## 2. Store Flow

1. Receive routed order.
2. Accept or reject based on stock and capacity.
3. Pack order and update readiness.
4. Hand over to partner after verification.
5. Track settlement and store performance.

## 3. Delivery Partner Flow

1. Go online.
2. Receive assignment offer.
3. Navigate to store and confirm pickup.
4. Navigate to customer address.
5. Verify customer identity at doorstep.
6. Mark delivered or return to store if verification fails.

## 4. Admin Flow

1. Review store and partner onboarding.
2. Configure legal rules and service zones.
3. Monitor active orders and exceptions.
4. Resolve support cases and refunds.
5. Export reports and review analytics.
