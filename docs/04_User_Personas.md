# 04. User Personas

## 1. Adult Customer

- Urban adult who wants fast, discreet, legal alcohol delivery.
- Cares about speed, price clarity, reliability, and trust.
- Needs a premium experience with simple repeat ordering.

## 2. Store Owner or Store Manager

- Licensed retailer who wants incremental demand and efficient order handling.
- Cares about inventory accuracy, margins, acceptance speed, and payouts.
- Needs a simple dashboard with low operational overhead.

## 3. Delivery Partner

- Verified rider who completes pickup and doorstep delivery.
- Cares about earnings, clear routing, low idle time, and fair assignment logic.
- Needs a workflow that minimizes confusion and missed handovers.

## 4. Operations Admin

- Internal operator responsible for compliance, support, and business health.
- Cares about control, auditability, escalations, and reporting.
- Needs a reliable panel with precise status and intervention tools.
