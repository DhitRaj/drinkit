# 01. Product Vision

## 1. Vision

Build the most trusted, compliant, and premium quick-commerce alcohol experience in India, with the speed of Blinkit and the operational discipline of a regulated marketplace.

## 2. Mission

Help verified adults discover, order, track, and receive eligible alcobev products in minutes, while giving stores, delivery partners, and regulators a system that is transparent and auditable.

## 3. Product Principles

- Compliance is a product feature, not a backend detail.
- Every screen must reduce friction without weakening age or legal gating.
- The app should feel premium, calm, and fast.
- No state, city, or store rule should be hardcoded in the client.
- Every major flow must have a clear success, empty, loading, and error state.

## 4. Positioning

Drinkit is not just a delivery app. It is a regulated commerce platform with four surfaces: customer, store, delivery partner, and admin. The brand should communicate trust, speed, legality, and quality.

## 5. Business Outcome

- Build repeat purchase behavior in a high-AOV category.
- Increase store sell-through and reduce idle inventory.
- Create efficient last-mile operations for short delivery windows.
- Establish a compliance-first operating model that can scale city by city.
